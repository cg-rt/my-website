### Changing a live account without breaking billing

The account had more than ten years of accumulated configuration: unused organizational units and billing setups far more granular than anyone needed. The risk sat in the billing. Orders landing on the wrong invoice, or users losing access, right before the automatic monthly invoicing run, would have been very visible to the client.

So I built the plan around that date. I pulled all invoices and their orders on 27 July as the reference, restructured the account, pulled them again and cross-checked totals and orders invoice by invoice before the 30 July run, with a plan to restore the previous structure if anything didn’t match. Support knew what was changing and when, cut their client documentation from 80 pages to 15 and switched to the new processes at the right moment. I kept checking invoices and support cases for several months afterwards and reported the method and the results to the client and to our executives.
