### Making the data and workflows usable

I worked with the client to obtain and normalize its legacy documents and metadata, design custom fields and library structure, and configure client-specific workflows. I ran duplicate checks, exception reviews and QA on the data, libraries and workflows, and brought in product, business and support teams where needed. Where Excel wasn’t enough, I wrote C# scripts to split fields, normalize dates so day and month couldn’t be swapped on import, skip superseded versions and clean fields by rule.

This was a separate engagement from the enterprise AI pilot.
