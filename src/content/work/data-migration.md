### Making the data and workflows usable

The client’s legacy documents and metadata had to be collected and normalized before anything could be imported. Excel handled part of that. Where it wasn’t enough, I wrote C# scripts to split fields, normalize dates so day and month couldn’t be swapped on import, skip superseded versions and clean fields by rule. I designed the custom fields and the library structure with the client and then configured their workflows. I ran duplicate checks and exception reviews on the data, and QA on the libraries and workflows. When a question belonged to product, business or support, I brought them in.
