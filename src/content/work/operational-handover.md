### Preparing the people as well as the platform

The client needed a content-management solution that fitted its internal workflows. I translated those requirements into a practical configuration and delivery sequence, using testing and client feedback to check the fit.

Training covered both users and administrators: one group needed to work with the platform, the other to support and maintain it. I connected that training with the documentation and knowledge transfer the operations team needed to take over.
