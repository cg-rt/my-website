### The situation

Following a twelve-month technical account review, a multinational enterprise needed to consolidate three large software accounts into one setup. The work involved approximately 30 stakeholders, different regional requirements, interpreters and multiple time zones.

This was as much a coordination problem as a configuration task. Different groups needed their requirements understood, and decisions had to hold across the whole implementation.

### My responsibility

I led the consolidation, coordinating requirements alignment, configuration decisions, quality assurance and client sign-off. I worked with the stakeholders involved to establish who needed to contribute to a decision and who needed to approve it.

I kept decisions and actions visible, coordinated communication across time zones, and worked through conflicting regional requirements with the relevant groups. The aim was to give everyone a shared understanding of the configuration and what remained to be done.

The earlier account review provided the groundwork for the integration. It connected a detailed understanding of the existing setup to the practical decisions needed to bring the three accounts together.

### What changed

The three accounts were consolidated into a single, fully working account. Stakeholders signed off and the project closed with no outstanding issues.

The useful lesson was practical: complex implementations need clear decision ownership and sustained follow-through alongside the technical work. Keeping those visible helped the different groups move through the consolidation together.
