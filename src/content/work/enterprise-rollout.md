### Working through the delivery constraints

The client was evaluating an AI-powered scientific-literature platform under licensed-content constraints. I structured the pilot around incremental delivery and feedback, with demonstrations, workshops, Q&A and train-the-trainer sessions.

When the AI capability fell short of expectations, I brought the client's requirements together with Product, the business unit and Engineering. We agreed what could be delivered within the content constraints and shortened the QA and demonstration cycle so the client could assess working software.

The July 2026 launch date was maintained. The implementation approach, tracking framework and presentation materials became reusable assets for colleagues. My responsibility was implementation and cross-functional delivery, not model development.
